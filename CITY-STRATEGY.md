# City strategy and automatic businesses — 1.28.0

Business policies apply per owned personal business type, to every copy. Stable: 1x and no extra inspection. Illegal: 1.4x gross, 6% hourly inspection, half that hour seized and one closed hour. Laundering: 2x gross, 12% hourly inspection, 80% seized and two closed hours. A closed business cannot receive another inspection until it reopens. A persistent secret seed and policy start produce repeatable hourly outcomes; refresh/restart cannot reroll. Changes first settle the old policy and require 24 hours. Partial minutes, custody pauses, stored income and the authoritative 24-active-hour collection cap remain intact. Employees are unaffected. Dashboard rates are gross; policy records show generated income and withheld/closure losses.

Four city sectors replace the old three-sector catalog. Commercial districts convert to industry/finance without changing plot IDs, owners, captured provenance, acquisition times or shields. Migration is transactional and versioned. Unowned plots are redistributed; other owned plots retain their type. Existing old aggregate quantities are allocated before normal reconciliation. All fifteen cities retain their geographic outlines and plot counts. Purchased/conquered limits remain 25+10 per city.

Family members and family production share their family's local holdings. Allies do not share economic perks. No worldwide sum of city percentages is used. Source and destination harbor effects combine only for boat routes, including recovered vehicles. Existing shipments and production keep locked arrival/remaining times. New production offers and Luca recommendations use the actual reduced duration. Street bonuses apply to destination sale quotes and execution. Financial fees are shared through territory-perks.js: base crypto spread 1% each side, art 8%, vehicle commission 3%, rounded to basis points for auctions. Auction commission locks at listing; older auctions retain original rates. Listing fees/appraisal fees remain unchanged. Investments already have no fees; no artificial fee was added.

Luca has native five-language policy and live current-city perk explanations; production/crypto advice consumes the same quoted prices. Bots choose owned business policies via ordinary validation; their catalogs and territory targeting include all four types.

Optional future family monuments are not implemented here: a founder's hall, victory memorial or collection museum would be permanent visual capital projects showing family history, complementary to personal XP distinctions. They need not add income multipliers or daily chores.
