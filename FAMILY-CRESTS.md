# Family heraldry — 1.18.2

Eight sculpted coats of arms: Lion, Serpent, Eagle, Trident, Bull, Dragon, Crown and Wolf. Crown and Wolf now have full artwork in the same ornate gold style as the other six.

Every old stored crest ID maps to the corresponding new artwork automatically. No database rewrite or reselection is required. Don and Capo can still change the choice: My family → Edit family → Family crest → Save family settings.

Visible silhouette bounds are measured from alpha transparency and used by the SVG viewport. Every design fills the same fixed display rectangle, including the entire crown and bottom ornament. Original PNG files stay intact.

Bots select from the server-provided preset list using the ordinary family/preferences handler. Luca indexes the authoritative familyCrests export in English and Dutch. Permissions and existing selections are tested in isolated databases. No economy or role permissions changed.
