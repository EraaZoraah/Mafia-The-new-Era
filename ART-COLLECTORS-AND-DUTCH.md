# Art, collector cars, condition and Dutch — 16 October 2026

Implemented in the local project; this does not deploy to an external host.

- Art & antiquities under Finances: three heists, twelve individually illustrated objects, unique serials, paid authenticity appraisals, reproductions, collection bonuses/titles and automatic cash-backed auctions.
- Art auctions: 6/12/24 hours, reserve 25–300% of appraised value, 8% sale commission, 5% bid increases, anti-sniping and protected outbid refunds. No guaranteed buyer; a seller's full wallet safely delays settlement.
- Twenty extra stolen models (50 total) and eighteen showroom models (44 total), including the Mercedes-Benz 300 SL Roadster and rare classics. All 38 additions have dedicated photographs generated for the game; see art/collector-cars-contact.jpg.
- Stolen car condition is rolled independently: Worn ★ 55%, Used ★★ 25%, Good ★★★ 13%, Perfect ★★★★ 6%, Pristine ★★★★★ 1%. Model values are multiplied by 35/55/75/100/135%, then existing city/hour and Big Score factors. Existing vehicles migrate to Perfect to preserve their prior value. Condition persists through storage, shipping and auction sale. Purchased cars retain their separate garage system.
- Vehicle auctions are on Warehouses, below the collection. Store the car first; choose a positive reserve and 6/12/24 hours. Always-paid listing cost: 0.25% of its local condition quote, minimum $500, maximum $250,000. Successful sale commission: 3%. Listed cars cannot also be sold, shipped or exchanged. Five listings maximum. Each winning bid reserves cash and one warehouse bay. Anti-sniping: two minutes, at most thirty extra minutes. Outbid refunds remain protected if cash is full. Automatic settlement is idempotent and runs while players are offline. Reset/delete cancels affected auctions without losing another bidder's money.
- Dutch selectable above Log out; English remains the default. French, German and Spanish remain Coming soon. Ranks and user-authored messages/names remain unchanged. Dollar currency and English numeric punctuation remain identical in every language. Dictionaries cover UI, validation/results, catalogs, rules, FAQ, changelogs and shared guide facts. Audits are in art/dutch-completeness-audit.json. Screens reviewed in desktop and 390px mobile layouts.
- Bots use ordinary eligibility/resource checks for art, appraisals, car auctions and all previously registered gameplay commands. They do not have staff permissions, make payments or change credentials. A persisted real server failure triggers a private bug ticket; deduplication limits the same route to one ticket per seven days, with retry throttling and no support-recursion. Normal insufficient-resource errors are not bug reports. This detects server errors, not every visual or logical bug.
- Luca indexes the shared catalogs/rulebook, has English and Dutch responses and uses current account quotes for production/transport advice. Car auctions and condition have an explicit topic so they are not confused with art auctions. It is a local rule guide, not an external AI or a guarantee of a globally optimal strategy.
- Every future update must test bot coverage and Luca knowledge and end its response with their truthful status. Recorded in AGENTS.md.

Local verification: `npm test` (HTTP tests need permission to bind a temporary local port). No production databases are used. Start with `npm start` from the directory containing package.json. If port 3000 is occupied, use PowerShell `$env:PORT='3101'; npm start` or open the existing running server.

Deployment: stop the server, back up the existing data directory, replace code/public files from the update archive, preserve data and environment configuration, then restart Node. Migrations run on startup. Do not delete the live database or replace it with preview/test data. The /api/health build should read 2026-10-16-art-collectors-1.
